users, and may be incomplete or wrong. The bot can also match the wrong book. Covers,
blurbs and other book details belong to their publishers and authors; the bot shows them
only to say what a book is.

The bot shares one daily allowance of lookups with every server that uses it, so heavy
use in one place can stop it working everywhere. The operator may block a server or a
person from using the bot to protect it.
